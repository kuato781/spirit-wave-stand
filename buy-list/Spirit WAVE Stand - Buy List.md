# Spirit WAVE Stand - Buy List

The Spirit WAVE implementation reuses the proven SMUT™ pivot/detent hardware from the Spektrum iX14 V2 design. The case geometry and stand are radio-specific, but the insert and fastener stack is otherwise the same.

### Heated inserts
- [ ] **6 × M3 heat-set inserts**, approximately **5 mm OD × 5 mm long**
- [ ] **1 × M4 heat-set insert**, approximately **6 mm OD × 6 mm long**

### Ball plunger
- [ ] **1 × M4 × 0.7 spring-loaded ball plunger**
  - Approximately **8-9 mm overall threaded-body length**
  - Approximately **2.5 mm ball**
  - Tested hardware elastic range: **3-15 N**

### Bolts, washers and nuts
- [ ] **2 × M4 × 20 mm socket-head or button-head screws**
- [ ] **2 × M4 Nyloc nuts**
- [ ] **2 × M4 flat washers**
- [ ] **4 × M3 × 12 mm socket-head or button-head screws**
- [ ] **2 × M3 × 8 mm button-head screws**
- [ ] **2 × M3 flat washers**

### Optional
- [ ] Small amount of **removable, non-permanent threadlocker** (for example, blue Loctite) for the ball-plunger threads

> **Note:** The Spirit WAVE cases use the same insert and fastener arrangement as the proven iX14 V2 SMUT™ mechanism. The WAVE-specific changes are the handle-fitting case geometry and the stand/header geometry.
