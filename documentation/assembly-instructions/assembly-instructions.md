# Assembly Instructions - Spirit WAVE Stand SMUT™ System

[Download the printable three-stage assembly guide (PDF)](../exploded-view/SpiritWave-assembly-guide.pdf)

These instructions apply to the Spirit WAVE implementation of **SMUT™** - **S**ide **M**ount **U**nit **T**echnology. The mechanism uses independent left and right pivot assemblies clamped around the WAVE's round metal handle, then joins them with the Spirit WAVE stand/header assembly.

**Left and right are always described while looking at the front of the radio.**

> **Hardware status:** the pivot, detent and mounting hardware are carried forward from the physically validated iX14 SMUT™ mechanism. The Spirit WAVE uses radio-specific case geometry and a WAVE-specific stand/header while retaining the same pivot-stub, replaceable detent-ring and ball-plunger architecture.

## Insert locations

Install all heat-set inserts before mounting the cases to the radio.

- **4 × M3 inserts:** two reinforced cap-screw pockets in each case body
- **2 × M3 inserts:** one square stand-mounting block on each pivot stub
- **1 × M4 insert:** spring-plunger bore in the right detent case body

Use the alignment feature supplied with your heat-set tool, such as an insert-centering nub, whenever one is available. If your heat-set setup does not already provide an alignment solution, an appropriate screw may be threaded loosely into the insert to help keep it straight. Press the insert into its reinforced collar and allow the part to cool completely before applying load.

> **Ball-plunger insert collar:** because of the nature of the print and the location of the M4 plunger insert, the printed collar/opening may not seat perfectly cleanly. If needed, **lightly clean it with a reamer** before installing the insert. Remove only enough material to clean the opening; do not unnecessarily enlarge the bore.

## 1. Assemble the right detent pivot

1. Place the replaceable detent ring on the support face of the right detent pivot stub.
2. Align the asymmetric square key and recess. The ring should seat fully without force.
3. Orient the detent pockets toward the right case body and its ball-plunger bore.
4. Slide the right pivot stub over the case body's 7 mm pivot sleeve.
5. Install the M4 pivot screw through the case body, sleeve and stub.
6. Fit the M4 washer on the Nyloc side, then install the M4 Nyloc nut.
7. Tighten until the hardware is firmly seated. Confirm that the sleeve shoulder prevents the stub from being pinched and that the stub still rotates freely.

## 2. Assemble the left plain pivot

1. Slide the left plain pivot stub over the left case body's pivot sleeve.
2. Install the M4 pivot screw through the case body, sleeve and stub.
3. Fit the M4 washer on the Nyloc side, then install the M4 Nyloc nut.
4. Tighten until firmly seated and confirm smooth, free rotation.

## 3. Mount the case assemblies to the WAVE handle

[Stage 2: attach the case assemblies to the WAVE handle](../exploded-view/images/step2.png)

1. Place the completed left and right case assemblies around their corresponding sides of the Spirit WAVE round metal handle.
2. Fit one **common case cap** to each case body. The two caps are identical; there is no left/right cap assignment.
3. Orient each common cap with its **beveled edge facing away from the radio**.
4. Make sure the handle is fully seated in the curved channel in each case and cap.
5. Install two M3 × 12 screws through each cap.
6. Snug the four cap screws only enough to hold the assemblies in position while the stand/header is fitted and used to square both sides.

Washers are not required under the case-cap screws.

> **Normal cap fit:** after final tightening there will be a **visible gap** between each cap and case body. This is expected. Tighten until you feel a **good bite** and the assembly is secure on the handle; do not overtighten simply to force the cap flush.

## 4. Attach the Spirit WAVE stand

[Stage 3: attach, square and tighten the Spirit WAVE stand](../exploded-view/images/step3.png)

1. Align the stand/header's two keyed recesses with the square mounting blocks on the pivot stubs.
2. Seat both mounting blocks completely in the header recesses.
3. Install one M3 × 8 button-head screw and M3 washer through each header hole and into the insert in the corresponding pivot-stub block.
4. Leave both stand screws slightly loose during alignment.

The keyed mounting blocks carry the stand's shear and twisting loads; the M3 screws retain and clamp the connection.

## 5. Square and tighten the assembly

1. Push both handle-mounted case assemblies as far toward the radio body as they will naturally seat.
2. Use the attached stand/header to bring both pivot assemblies square and parallel.
3. Tighten the four M3 × 12 cap screws evenly, alternating between screws so each cap clamps uniformly around the handle.
4. Stop when the caps have a secure **good bite** on the handle. A visible cap-to-body gap is normal.
5. Tighten the two M3 × 8 stand screws and washers.
6. Fold and deploy the stand through its complete travel to confirm that neither side binds or shifts.

Do not overtighten the cap screws against the radio handle.

## 6. Install and adjust the ball plunger

1. Use something thin, such as an awl or straightened paper clip, to place a **small amount of removable, non-permanent threadlocker** inside the receiving threads of the M4 insert. Keep the threadlocker off the surrounding plastic and out of the plunger mechanism.
2. Insert the M4 × 0.7 spring-loaded ball plunger **ball end first** through the access bore, then thread it into the M4 insert in the right detent case body.
3. Advance the plunger a little at a time while repeatedly moving the stand between its fully folded and fully extended positions.
4. Continue making small adjustments until the ball engages the detent recesses solidly at each indexed position without making the pivot overly tight or difficult to move.
5. Cycle the stand repeatedly through its full range. Confirm positive engagement at every position and verify that the replaceable detent ring remains fully seated.

The correct setting is the point at which the detents feel deliberate and secure while the stand still moves smoothly. Do not use permanent threadlocker.

## Final inspection

- Both case assemblies are fully seated on the intended WAVE handle locations
- Both common caps have their beveled edges facing away from the radio
- Both common caps have a secure bite; a visible cap-to-body gap is acceptable and expected
- All four cap screws are evenly tightened
- Both pivot screws are secure with washers and Nyloc nuts
- Both pivots rotate freely without axial looseness
- Detent-ring key is fully seated and pockets face the plunger
- Ball plunger engages each detent recess positively without binding
- Both stand/header keyed recesses are fully seated on the pivot-stub mounting blocks
- Both stand screws have washers and are secure
- Stand deploys smoothly and supports the radio at the intended angle
- Stand folds without contacting the radio body, switches or controls
- Handle opening remains clear for normal carrying

## Transport / case fit

**The radio will not fit in the stock Spirit case with this stand installed unless you are willing to modify the case.**

The documented physical build is transported in a **Spektrum two-radio case**, which also works well when carrying multiple transmitters.

## Safety and maintenance

- This is a desk/simulator stand, not a replacement carrying handle.
- Inspect the printed parts, inserts and fasteners periodically for cracks, loosening or wear.
- Replace the detent ring if its pockets become rounded or inconsistent.
- Recheck cap and stand screws after the first several use cycles.
- Recheck the ball-plunger adjustment after the first several use cycles and whenever engagement begins to feel weak or excessively tight.
- Never force the stand beyond its designed range of motion.
- Use the stand at your own risk and verify stability before placing the radio on any elevated, uneven or windy surface.
